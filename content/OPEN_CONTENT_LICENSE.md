# MIT examination adaptations

The `OPEN_PAPER_QUESTIONS` items in questionBank.ts are adapted from selected questions in **5.111 Principles of Chemical Science, Fall 2014**, Catherine Drennan / Massachusetts Institute of Technology, published by MIT OpenCourseWare.

- [Exam 1](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/5-111sc-principles-of-chemical-science-fall-2014/resources/mit5_111f14_exam1/) and [solutions](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/5-111sc-principles-of-chemical-science-fall-2014/resources/mit5_111f14_exam1sol/)
- [Exam 2](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/5-111sc-principles-of-chemical-science-fall-2014/resources/mit5_111f14_exam2/) and [solutions](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/5-111sc-principles-of-chemical-science-fall-2014/resources/mit5_111f14_exam2sol/)

The questions were shortened, converted to four-choice questions, and supplied with new distractors and explanatory wording. The source reference appears on every adapted item.
