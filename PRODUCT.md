# CarbonCanvas

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Users
Chemistry students studying Cambridge 9701 (2025–2027), current IB Diploma chemistry, and introductory undergraduate chemistry. These course choices are confirmed by the user's request and their Cambridge preference.

## Product Purpose
Connect syllabus study, active recall, molecular structures, and reaction reasoning in one workspace. Readers must be able to distinguish cited educational content from AI predictions.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Existing React 19 / TypeScript / Vite application. Preserve the study hub, flashcards, quizzes, molecule inspection and editing, archive, two-reactant simulation, mechanism tutor, and chat tutor. Gemini remains the configured AI service. Progress and saved molecules are local to this browser. There is no experimental reaction engine or measured 3D geometry database.

## Brand Commitments
Keep the CarbonCanvas name. The user selected a scientific workspace and requested a distinctive palette, spacing, and shading. Exact tokens belong to DESIGN.md.

## Evidence on Hand
Cambridge official 9701 syllabus and IB official Structure/Reactivity curriculum. OpenStax chemistry textbooks for foundational facts; ZNotes and Save My Exams as linked revision resources. RDKit for graph sanitization and depiction. Do not call AI output experimentally verified or imply third-party skill links are executable integrations.

## Product Principles
Show sources next to learning content. Preserve formal charge and stereochemistry. Separate 2D drawing conventions from physical bond angles. Make every syllabus module readable without requiring an AI call.
