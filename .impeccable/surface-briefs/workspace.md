# Workspace redesign

Mode: operate; reading surfaces use read mode within the same shell.

## Direction contract
THESIS: Chemistry study and molecule exploration share one familiar workspace. The user selected the scientific workspace rather than the other offered directions.
OWN-WORLD: Mineral #edf1f0, paper #ffffff, petrol #173d42, ink #233738, copper #956144, muted #586b6c. Source Sans 3 for readable UI and headings. Soft downward elevation belongs to the workbench, fine borders to lists. Light reading surfaces suit long study sessions under normal daylight.
STORY: Choose a course, open a source-linked module, recall its terms, then inspect the relevant structure or reaction. Distinguish reference content, graph validation, and AI predictions.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Persistent petrol rail; compact course selector in a white top bar; a large left-aligned study heading; asymmetric current-module work area with a real molecular preview at right; course rows below. Primary action opens the current module. Signature interaction connects named geometry facts to the molecule selected in the workbench.
FORM: Scientific reference atlas translated into a standard web workspace. Seven considered sources: textbook marginalia, chemical atlas, laboratory notebook, journal figures, museum collection index, periodic classification, and seminar notes. User-pinned scientific workspace governs the design. seed: none (unavailable; the command returned a session key but no durable seed reference).
FINISH: Finish review ACCEPT after stale-request fixes; DESIGN.md and .impeccable/design.json document implemented tokens and components. Shipping molecular images are runtime RDKit SVGs. Preview JPEGs are screenshots of the implemented app, not generated assets.
